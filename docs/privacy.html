Second Wind privacy policy

Applies to the Second Wind Windows app, browser button, desktop helper and Claude Code plugin. Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version

Second Wind doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings: favorites, your usual drink, your lifetime drink count, where you put the floating can, and on/off switches. They are saved only on your device.
  • When you hand over a drink, the app puts the drink message on your clipboard, pastes it into the chat app you were using, then puts your old clipboard text back. It doesn't read, keep or send anything else from your clipboard or your screen.

Network

The Windows app from the Microsoft Store makes no network requests (the Store handles updates). The browser button and the desktop helper only download one small public file from GitHub to check if a new version is out.

Questions
