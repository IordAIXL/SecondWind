SECOND WIND FOR DESKTOP APPS (Windows 10 and 11)

Hand any AI desktop app a pretend energy drink: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot,
Perplexity, or anything with a chat box. Nothing to install.

START IT
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example Documents).
  2. Double-click "Start Second Wind.bat".
     (Windows may hide the ".bat" part, so it can show as just "Start Second Wind"
     with a gear icon. Its type is "Windows Batch File".)
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     No .bat file at all? Right-click "SecondWind.ps1" and pick "Run with PowerShell".
  3. A purple can appears in your tray, near the clock (it may be under the ^ arrow).

USE IT
  Click into the chat box of your AI app, then:
    Ctrl+Alt+D   pick a drink from the can menu
    Ctrl+Alt+U   hand over your usual drink instantly (one key press)
  The drink message lands in the chat box. Press Enter to send it,
  or turn on "Send right away" to skip that.

FAVORITES
  Right-click any flavor can to star it or make it your usual.
  Starred drinks show up at the top of the menu and in the tray menu.
  Your usual is the gold one. Made-up flavors can be starred too.

TRAY MENU (right-click the tray can)
  Give my usual, your other favorites, Pick a drink, Send right away,
  Start with Windows, and Quit.

Settings and your lifetime count are saved in %APPDATA%\SecondWind\settings.json.
Second Wind is a fan-made joke, not affiliated with any drink brand.

UPDATES
  Second Wind checks GitHub for a new version and updates itself,
  keeping your favorites and count. Turn this off with
  "Install updates automatically" in the tray menu, or pick
  "Check for updates" any time.
